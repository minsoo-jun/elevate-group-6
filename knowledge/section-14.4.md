# Section 14.4 — Pre-Approval Requirements

**Parent:** SECTION 14: COMMERCIAL GIFTS & ENTERTAINMENT (NON-GOVERNMENT)

**14.4 Pre-Approval Requirements**

Business courtesies of more than US$100 per person in value require written pre-approval because they may create an appearance of impropriety.

* **Under US$100:** No pre-approval is required.
* **Over US100 upto US250:** Requires written pre-approval from the employee's manager.
* **Over US250 upto US500:** Requires written pre-approval from the employee's director (or country manager if there is no director in-country).
* **Over US$500:** Requires written pre-approval from the employee's VP.
