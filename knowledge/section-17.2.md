# Section 17.2 — Definition of a Flexible Working Request

**Parent:** SECTION 17: FLEXIBLE WORK REQUESTS (SINGAPORE)

**17.2 Definition of a Flexible Working Request**

A flexible working request under this policy generally means a request to do any or all of the following:

* Reduce or vary your working hours.
* Reduce or vary the days you work.
* Work from a different location (for example, from home).
* Any combination of the above arrangements.
